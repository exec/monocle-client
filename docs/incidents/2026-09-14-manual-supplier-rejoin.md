# Manual supplier rejoin

Resuming a displaced supplier against its old supply origin can block it even when no physical supply session remains. Module activation and host Resume share the existing supplier-return path when the original world and live crew are valid.

The transition requires no host pause or cancellation, active exchange, tracked container/drop, pending block acknowledgement or physical cleanup. Movement resumes before normal work, and visible crew or a fresh host front guides return. Ownership and recovery records are preserved.
