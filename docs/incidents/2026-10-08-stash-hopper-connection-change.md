# Changing hopper connections during pickup

A cached hopper chain can differ from the next loaded-world lookup because connections changed or previously unknown blocks became visible. That difference alone must not claim source exhaustion.

Refresh the live column and opening target, reset refill/navigation waits and obtain fresh server contents. Bound consecutive no-progress refreshes and reset the budget on confirmed pickup. Repeatedly unstable solo sources defer so other candidates can continue.

Lost upstream evidence remains uncertain. Refresh cannot discard an unconfirmed inventory move or redirect a crew-local lease. Diagnostics retain both old and new chains; tests cover newly visible and lost links, pending-operation refusal, checkpoints and deferral semantics.
