# Incidental kits during collection

An unrelated box occupying the intended player destination does not confirm the requested pickup. It may result from ground collection or source changes; that cause cannot be inferred solely from the occupied slot.

Fresh-menu recovery may allow additive non-requested shulkers only when requested cargo is unchanged and cursors are empty. Known newly acquired extras can be deposited separately; unknown extras use an exact confirmed discard operation. Pre-job personal boxes remain protected.

Side deposits have separate confirmed counts. A partial load caused by incidental inventory is not source exhaustion. Changed cargo, ambiguous clicks and unsupported crew leases retain their safety checks.
