# Bulk deposit recovery and incidental inventory

Positive bulk receipts require exact planned-kit inventory changes and destination evidence. Unrelated non-shulker changes, including food consumption or incidental items in emptied player slots, need not invalidate the receipt; every other shulker remains protected.

Recovery requires a different accepted menu, full authoritative contents matching local inventory and empty cursors. Remaining planned sources must hold their original kit or be empty. Absent cargo without destination proof is an explicit missing outcome, never a successful deposit. Unchanged cargo can be selected anew after the ordering barrier; the old click is not replayed.

Recovery disables bulk mode for that action, refreshes destination observations and continues with exact-slot moves. Missing counts persist through checkpoints. Withdrawals retain stricter destination checks. Per-worker home timers remain independent and must not inherit a leader's shorter cooldown.
