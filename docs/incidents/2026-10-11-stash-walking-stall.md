# Walking stalls and flight fallback

Near-target flight selection must consider lower as well as elevated chests. A non-destructive walking lease may not have any route to a lower or top-row destination.

After bounded no-closer-progress, eligible stash navigation tries existing collision-checked flight. A separate wall-clock watchdog covers stopped entity ticks and can defer a safe solo until-empty source; fixed-count and crew-local tasks, pending transfers and held cursors are excluded.

Death or lobby readiness must be checked before interpreting a timeout as an inaccessible source. A failed direct DNS A lookup is not sufficient diagnosis for a Minecraft server with SRV records. Runtime evidence must distinguish alive-player state, connection readiness and actual approach progress.
