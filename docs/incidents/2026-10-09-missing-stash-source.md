# Removed sources and rescan reconciliation

A rescan that only merges encountered coordinates can retain an old chest after it becomes air. Withdrawal invalidations may intentionally remain searchable, so retrying the same stale manifest does not repair catalog knowledge.

Workers skip a persistently non-storage source only after loaded-world rechecks. Unloaded chunks, not-yet-loaded storage entities, cursor items and uncertain clicks retain their gates. Confirmed partial pickups remain deliverable.

The catalog correction uses deduplicated revision-checked live observations and explicit removals. Removed containers leave stock, capacity and plans; unopened new storage contributes capacity with unknown contents. Double-chest aliases and surviving halves reconcile separately. Missing observations in partial scans cannot erase stock by omission. See the live-observation section of the stash guide.
