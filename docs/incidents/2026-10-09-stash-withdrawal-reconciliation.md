# Withdrawal verification and recovery

An unchanged carried count and empty destination do not prove a pickup. Pending withdrawal recovery needs authoritative source, destination and cursor evidence plus a fresh menu boundary before any reselection.

Preserve confirmed earlier pickups and bound reopening attempts. Exact successful receipts retire intent once; unchanged or cursor-returned cargo can be reconciled only under the existing conservation checks. Missing or conflicting cargo remains uncertain rather than becoming an automatic duplicate click.

Detailed transaction diagnostics are necessary to distinguish server rejection, ordering and unrelated inventory updates. Operational journals and captured packets belong outside the repository.
