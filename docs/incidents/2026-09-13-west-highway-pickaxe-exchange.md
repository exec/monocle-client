# Pickaxe receipts and supply-site clearance

A durable uncertain exchange can outlive its transient retry state. A donor's inventory exchange may also prevent native travel and leave that donor blocking another worker's placement area. Visible status churn must not reset effective no-progress detection.

An uncertain issued drop cannot be replayed or declared recovered from a current empty exchange snapshot. Cancellation must retain the receipt and complete physical cleanup before replacement work. Investigate exchange, travel, reservation and clearance gates separately.

Private operational traces are not retained in this document; current runtime evidence is required before resolving an outstanding receipt.
