# Server wall damage and personal food reserves

Full hunger does not establish usable carried food. Personal resupply policy must count inventory that Auto Eat can actually use and retain its reserve checks.

Server-reported wall damage can disagree with a locally clear swept route. Stash flight feedback brakes movement, lands on verified support and temporarily prefers walking before retrying flight with its existing clearance margin. Player/world identity scopes packet handling.

This does not establish the exact server collision cause or replace durability and food guards. Navigation recovery, inventory reconciliation and personal-supply failure must be diagnosed independently.
